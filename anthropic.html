Anthropic's frontier ECI and the benchmark scores behind it

Coloured lines are the benchmark scores of the 17 Claude models that set a new Anthropic best ECI (named along the top), in log-odds (left axis). The thick line is Anthropic's frontier ECI (right axis), with its 90% interval; the dashed grey line is the overall frontier across all labs. Colour runs from the oldest benchmark (blue) to the newest (red), matching the all-labs chart.

The interactive chart could not load here, so this is the static version.

Data: Epoch AI's own ECI fit input (processed_data_for_eci.csv) and published ECI scores (eci_scores.csv) from benchmark_data.zip, snapshot of 1 Oct 2026, filtered to the 26 Anthropic models in the ECI. Anthropic frontier ECI is the running maximum of those models' ECI by release date. The 17 record-setting models contribute of the Anthropic scores, on 53 benchmarks. ECIs come from Epoch's joint fit over every lab's models, so they are directly comparable with the overall frontier.

The slope-matched right axis is scaled so a benchmark with the median fitted slope (0.107 logits per ECI point) runs parallel to the ECI line; steeper benchmarks climb faster than it. Scores of exactly 0 or 1 are pinned to 1% and 99%. Data CC BY 4.0, Epoch AI.
